# Suspicious or phishing e-mail

Symptom: "is this mail real?", "I got a suspicious link", "I clicked something".

1. analyze_email: sender, links and attachments; verdict safe / suspicious / phishing.
2. Phishing: quarantine_email (removes it from every mailbox) and block_sender.
3. If the employee clicked the link or typed a password: reset the password (SOP L1/002-password-reset) and hand the device to L2 (possible malware).
4. Send a short awareness note: "Don't open mails from the same source; forward them to us."
