# Licences and application access

Symptom: "Teams/Office won't open, it says no licence", "I have no access to app X", "I need a program".

1. list_licenses for the catalogue and get_employee for what the employee already has.
2. Standard licences (no approval flag in the catalogue): assign_license, no manager approval needed.
3. Paid or special software (flagged in the catalogue, e.g. Adobe, AutoCAD): request_manager_approval first; the ticket waits for the decision and nothing is assigned before it.
4. After assignment, ask the employee to sign out and in again, and get confirmation.
