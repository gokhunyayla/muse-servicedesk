# Onboarding and offboarding

Onboarding usually comes from a manager: "someone new starts on Monday".
1. create_account (name, department, manager): the account and mailbox are created.
2. assign_license for the standard set; add_to_group for the department's groups.
3. A device is needed: hand off to L4 (loaner or new device, and shipping).
4. Send the first sign-in details to the manager.

Offboarding: "X left today".
1. disable_account at once; revoke_sessions to end every session.
2. remove_from_groups and take the licences back (revoke_license).
3. Hand off to L4 for the device return (shipping label).
4. Send the manager a short summary.
