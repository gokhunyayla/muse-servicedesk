# Hardware failure, service case and shipping

1. check_warranty: under warranty → vendor service; otherwise a loaner plus a note for the repair-or-scrap decision.
2. open_service_case with a summary: symptom, stop code or error, what was tried.
3. assign_loaner so the employee can keep working.
4. create_shipment: the loaner to the employee, the faulty device to service. Write the tracking number on the ticket.
5. schedule_followup every 2 days to check the delivery with the employee.
6. When the repaired device is back, arrange the loaner's return (create_shipment) and add_note it.
